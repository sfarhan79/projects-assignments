Decision Quant: Strategic Decision Support Framework

This project provides a quantitative framework for predictive business analysis, leveraging data science to transform traditional financial metrics into actionable insights for risk management and strategic project evaluation.

🚀 Overview
The "Decision Quant" framework is designed to move beyond intuition-based decision-making. It utilizes a weighted quantitative model to evaluate project viability and business risk, providing a clear "Go/No-Go" logic for complex investment scenarios.

🛠️ Key Capabilities
* Weighted Multi-Criteria Analysis:** Aggregates qualitative and quantitative inputs into a single, cohesive decision score.
* Sensitivity Modeling:** Allows for stress-testing business cases against changing market assumptions.
* Strategic Risk Assessment:** Bridges the gap between raw data and executive-level decision support.

🤖 Development Note
This tool was architected through an AI-assisted development workflow. The core logic was defined to meet specific financial requirements and was rigorously validated for accuracy and business application.
